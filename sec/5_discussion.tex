\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

STAGS uses a single Gaussian mixture throughout an STA pipeline, from pose search to polishing. On EMPIAR-10164, its cross-FSC crossings are close to RELION-5's half-map FSC crossing, while the recorded STAGS run is shorter. The runs used different hardware and processing scopes.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
STAGS's own half-map FSC remains above 0.143 through Nyquist, so it does not provide a usable gold-standard resolution estimate. We therefore report cross-FSC agreement with RELION's half maps. RELION also agrees better with the atomic model (FSC~0.5 at 3.86\,\AA\ versus 4.04\,\AA\ for STAGS).
