\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

STAGS's own half-map FSC remains above 0.143 through Nyquist, so it does not provide a usable gold-standard resolution estimate. We therefore report cross-FSC agreement with RELION's half maps. RELION also agrees better with the atomic model (FSC~0.5 at 3.86\,\AA\ versus 4.04\,\AA\ for STAGS).

Future work should establish an intrinsic resolution estimate for Gaussian maps, evaluate fully independent half-sets, and test in situ data.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

STAGS uses a single Gaussian mixture, projected analytically through its closed-form Fourier transform, as the reference for every stage of an STA pipeline, from pose search to map fitting and polishing.
On EMPIAR-10164, starting from the initial picks, its cross-FSC crossings are close to RELION-5's half-map FSC crossing in a shorter recorded run, while RELION's map agrees better with the atomic model.
With poses fixed, fitting Gaussians to the tilt images gives better maps than voxel reconstruction from the same particles, and the controls support image-level fitting as a source of this gain.
These results suggest that a compact, differentiable map representation is a practical basis for STA, and that resolution estimation for Gaussian maps and evaluation beyond a single benchmark are the main steps that remain.
