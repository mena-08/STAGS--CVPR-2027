\section{Run time, run-to-run variation and speed}
\label{sec:suppl:timing}
% tables: scripts/paper_supp_timing.py (numbers docs/paper/supp_timing_numbers.json); speed numbers: scripts/paper_supp_speed.py (docs/paper/supp_speed_numbers.json)

We ran the adopted recipe twice, blind from the initial picks, each half-set on its own GPU: run~1 on $2\times$ RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q (96\,GB each; Xeon Silver 4510, 125\,GB RAM), run~2 on $2\times$ RTX 4090 (24\,GB each; Threadripper PRO 3975WX, 251\,GB RAM).

Picks to final map take 4.27\,h and 4.39\,h (\cref{tab:suppl:stages}); run~2 needed four launches,\footnote{Stops on a missing configuration file, Triton kernels compiled for the wrong GPU and a hard-coded path; the first launch stopped before any timing. A third GPU (RTX 5070 Ti) computed only the untimed reporting scores.} and its clock credits the stages finished before each interruption.

\paragraph{Same-hardware comparison with RELION-5.}
On $2\times$ RTX 4090, RELION-5~\cite{burt2024relion5} reports 13\,h\,04\,min from the picks to its first bin-1 map (4.0\,\AA) and 3\,h\,22\,min per polishing cycle, ${\sim}29.9$\,h for the five cycles that reach 3.3\,\AA\ (our sum; times from their table, not re-measured).
% src: docs/relion5-paper-table4.md (13 h 4 min; 3 h 22 min; 512 GB RAM; 4.0 A, 3.3 A after five iterations); docs/paper/supp_timing_numbers.json (relion_table4_h.five_cycles_total_our_sum 29.9)
STAGS's 4.39\,h is $3.0\times$ and $6.8\times$ shorter, with half their host RAM (251 vs.\ 512\,GB).
% src: docs/paper/supp_hardware.txt (251 GB); docs/paper/supp_timing_numbers.json (wall_h.2 4.387; ratio_relion_over_stages_4090 2.98 / 6.82)

\paragraph{Run-to-run variation.}
The runs differ only through reduction order and the GPU-dependent random grid sampling of the map fits, so run~2 is an independent realization.
Their crossings differ by 0.025\,\AA\ (half~1) and 0.007\,\AA\ (half~2), and half~1's margin to RELION-5 changes sign between runs (\cref{tab:suppl:runs}).
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_numbers.json (run_spread_A 0.0252 / 0.0066; margin_to_job078_A_positive_STAGS_finer: run1 0.0143/0.0332, run2 -0.0109/0.0398); docs/paper/supp_timing_runs.csv
We therefore read the crossings as parity with RELION-5, which keeps its lead in FSC at 3.5\,\AA, band mean and atomic agreement.
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_runs.csv (fsc35, band, atomic05_A of runs 1, 2 and job078)

\paragraph{Speed limit.}
Gaussian map fitting is the largest kind of work (83--85\,min per half in run~1).
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_kinds.csv (map, pro6000_h1/h2: 84.6/82.6)
Each KB map-fit update evaluates all $K=48{,}000$ Gaussians at up to 262{,}144 sampled frequencies: $1.26\times10^{10}$ Gaussian--frequency pairs forward and again backward.
% src: runs/engine/supp_speed_20261121/profile/p3_base_prof.log (argv --K 48000; 'grid-frequency samples <=262144/update'); stags/train.py (KB objective sample_size=262144); docs/paper/supp_speed_numbers.json (profile.pairs 12582912000)
The two kernels take 97.7\% of the GPU time and are arithmetic-bound by design (exponential, sine and cosine per pair).
% src: docs/paper/supp_speed_numbers.json (profile.kernel_share_of_gpu 0.977); runs/engine/supp_speed_20261121/profile/p3_base_prof_cuda_top.txt; runs/engine/supp_speed_20261121/profile/p3_base_prof_gpu_timeline.json; stags/fourier_volume_triton.py (tl.exp, sin.approx.f32/cos.approx.f32); no hardware counters collected
Four exact implementation changes (bitwise-identical P3 updates) gain only 1.6--2.2\% in it/s; no reported run used them.
% src: runs/engine/supp_speed_20261121/profile/pro_bench_eq.txt (P3 27.98 -> 28.43, cold fit 27.94 -> 28.56 it/s); runs/engine/supp_speed_20261121/profile/pro_eq_compare.json (history_bitwise_equal True, model bitwise True, B_4536.mrc bitwise True); runs/engine/supp_speed_20261121/profile/speed_profile.diff; docs/paper/supp_speed_profile.csv (bench_*_rate_gain)
The ${\sim}200$\,it/s of real-space 3D Gaussian splatting would need ${\sim}7\times$ fewer pair evaluations, a recipe change (fewer sampled frequencies, or splatting onto the padded grid with one FFT) that we did not test.
% src: docs/paper/supp_speed_numbers.json (profile.unprofiled_rate 28.28; 200/28.28 = 7.07); 200 it/s is the rate quoted to us for 3DGS-style fits, not measured here

\paragraph{Variants.}
Two predeclared variants each completed once on run~1's machine (\cref{tab:suppl:stages,tab:suppl:runs}).
% src: engine_rules.json steps.supp_speed_20261121 (variants, question, reporting, limitations, incidents)
\texttt{-{}-fit\_scale 0.5} halves the updates of the cold fit and the four P3 fits; it saves 10.1\,min, and no final-map value is worse than both runs by more than 0.001.
% src: docs/paper/supp_speed_numbers.json (saving_vs_run1_min.fit05 10.09; spread_check.fit05: one inside, five better, half-2 FSC at 3.5 A worse by 0.001)
\texttt{-{}-k\_scale 0.5} halves every Gaussian budget and saves 26.2\,min, but its FSC at 3.5\,\AA\ and band mean fall below both runs and its atomic curve dips near 15\,\AA.
% src: docs/paper/supp_speed_numbers.json (saving_vs_run1_min.k05 26.22; spread_check.k05: fsc35 -0.025/-0.025, band -0.005/-0.020; runs.k05.atomic_detail); engine_rules.json steps.supp_speed_20261121.variants.k_half
Its seed stage needs 24 instead of 9 rounds on half~1, so the stage-time gate aborted the first two attempts; the reported third attempt only widens the stage budgets.
% src: docs/paper/supp_speed_numbers.json (k_scale_seed: rounds half 1 24 in all three attempts vs 9 in run 1; aborted[*].abort: step2_16_12 half 2 stage time > 1.5 x budget); runs/engine/supp_speed_20261121/k_scale_0.5/launch.json (--ladder_budget); engine_rules.json steps.supp_speed_20261121.incidents (correction: not GPU contention)
With one run each, neither variant changes the recipe of the main paper.
