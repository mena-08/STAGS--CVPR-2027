\section{Run time and run-to-run variation}
\label{sec:suppl:timing}

We ran the adopted recipe twice, blind from the initial picks, on two machines.
Run~1 used a workstation with $2\times$ RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q Workstation Edition (96\,GB each), a 48-thread Xeon Silver 4510 and 125\,GB RAM.
Run~2 used a workstation with $2\times$ RTX 4090 (24\,GB each), a 32-core Threadripper PRO 3975WX and 251\,GB RAM.
% src: docs/paper/supp_hardware.txt (lscpu, free -g, nvidia-smi of both hosts)
A third GPU in the second workstation (RTX 5070 Ti) computed only the reporting scores, which do not count toward the run time.
% src: docs/paper/supp_hardware.txt; runs/engine/abc_4090_20261117/launch.json (machine_env STAGS_SCORE_GPU=0, STAGS_HALF_GPUS=1,2)
In both runs each half-set runs on its own GPU.
The wall clock from the picks to the final map is 256.2\,min (4.27\,h) in run~1 and 263.2\,min (4.39\,h) in run~2 (\cref{tab:suppl:stages}).
% src: runs/engine/pose_abc_adopt_20261113/blind/report.json (wall_min 256.2256); runs/engine/abc_4090_20261117/full_logs/report.json (wall_min 263.1905); docs/paper/supp_timing_numbers.json (wall_min, wall_h)
Run~2 was launched four times.
The first two launches stopped at start-up (a missing configuration file; Triton kernels compiled for the wrong GPU), and the third crashed in the first optics scan on a hard-coded path.
% src: runs/engine/abc_4090_20261117/run_abc-4090.log (ABORTED lines at +1 min, +4 min, +131 min; cycle 1 scan failed); causes as recorded by the operator
The first launch stopped after 1\,min, before any timing was recorded, and is not counted.
% src: runs/engine/abc_4090_20261117/run_abc-4090.log (launched 07:34:57Z, ABORTED 07:35:52Z; partial launch.json/attempts.json moved aside on resume)
The clock of the remaining three launches is stitched: stages finished before an interruption are credited with their measured time, and partially done work and the interruptions themselves are excluded (footnote of \cref{tab:suppl:stages}).
% src: runs/engine/abc_4090_20261117/full_logs/report.json (resume); runs/engine/abc_4090_20261117/attempts.json

\paragraph{Where the time goes.}
\Cref{tab:suppl:kinds} splits each half's timeline by kind of work, using the stages' own logs.
Gaussian map fitting takes 83--85\,min per half on the PRO 6000 and 73--74\,min on the RTX 4090.
Pose search takes 46--47\,min on the PRO 6000 and 72--73\,min on the RTX 4090.
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_kinds.csv (map: 84.6/82.6, 72.6/74.0; pose: 46.3/47.0, 73.3/72.3)
The optics scans take 34 and 42\,min, P1 26--27 and 32\,min, and CPU extraction and preparation 29 and 26\,min.
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_kinds.csv (scan 33.7/33.9, 42.1/41.9; p1 26.8/26.2, 31.6/31.6; cpu 29.1/28.6, 26.5/26.4)
Loading data inside the stages takes 22--30\,min per half on the first machine and 12\,min on the second.
On the first machine, the optics scans' data reloads alone take 16.7 and 9.3\,min on the two halves.
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_kinds.csv (data 30.1/22.0, 12.1/11.7); docs/paper/supp_timing_numbers.json (kinds.run1_h1/h2.scan_data 16.73/9.30)
The rest is time a half waits for the other half or for the orchestrator: 4.6 and 14.3\,min in run~1 and 3.9 and 4.0\,min in run~2.
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_kinds.csv (idle)

\paragraph{Same-hardware comparison with RELION-5.}
The RELION-5 paper~\cite{burt2024relion5} reports times for the same dataset on $2\times$ RTX 4090 with a 32-core Threadripper PRO 5975WX and 512\,GB RAM: 13\,h\,04\,min from the picks to the first bin-1 map at 4.0\,\AA, and 3\,h\,22\,min per polishing cycle.
That is about 29.9\,h for the five cycles that reach 3.3\,\AA\ (our sum, assuming every cycle takes as long as the one in their table).
% src: docs/relion5-paper-table4.md (13 h 4 min; 3 h 22 min; 512 GB RAM; 4.0 A, 3.3 A after five iterations); docs/paper/supp_timing_numbers.json (relion_table4_h.five_cycles_total_our_sum 29.9)
These RELION-5 times are taken from that table; we did not measure them.
On the same GPU model, with half the host RAM (251 vs.\ 512\,GB), STAGS takes 4.39\,h from the picks to its final map: a factor of 3.0 below RELION-5's time to its first bin-1 map and 6.8 below its estimated time to 3.3\,\AA.
% src: docs/paper/supp_hardware.txt (251 GB); docs/paper/supp_timing_numbers.json (wall_h.2 4.387; ratio_relion_over_stages_4090 2.98 / 6.82)
To fit the 24\,GB cards, the observations stay in host-pinned memory and stream to the GPU, and every stage sizes its chunks from a per-process budget (here capped at 22\,GB).
% src: stags/relion5_stags.py (pin_memory store); stags/gpu_budget.py (STAGS_GPU_MEM_GB cap, budget_bytes for chunk sizing); runs/engine/abc_4090_20261117/launch.json (STAGS_GPU_MEM_GB 22)
In the ladder, peak memory per half is then 19.4--19.5\,GB, versus 46.5--46.6\,GB without the cap on the PRO 6000.
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_numbers.json (peak_gpu_GB_per_half), from lean/logs/ladder_h{1,2}.log 'peak GPU' lines of both runs
The computation is the same, only in smaller pieces.
The results can differ only through the order of reductions and through the random grid sampling of the map fits, whose CUDA random stream depends on the GPU model.
Run~2 is therefore an independent random realization of the same pipeline, not a copy of run~1.

\paragraph{Run-to-run variation.}
\Cref{fig:suppl:runs,tab:suppl:runs} compare the final maps of both runs.
The crossings are 3.269/3.251\,\AA\ in run~1 and 3.294/3.245\,\AA\ in run~2, so they differ between runs by 0.025\,\AA\ on half~1 and 0.007\,\AA\ on half~2.
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_runs.csv; docs/paper/supp_timing_numbers.json (run_spread_A 0.0252 / 0.0066)
Against RELION-5 (3.284/3.284\,\AA), half~1's margin changes sign between runs: STAGS is finer by 0.014\,\AA\ in run~1 and coarser by 0.011\,\AA\ in run~2.
Half~2 is finer than RELION-5 in both runs, by 0.033 and 0.040\,\AA.
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_numbers.json (crossing_job078 3.2836/3.2844; margin_to_job078_A_positive_STAGS_finer: run1 0.0143/0.0332, run2 -0.0109/0.0398)
With two runs on different GPUs, we cannot tell whether half~2's margin is a stable difference specific to that half.
We therefore read the crossings as parity with RELION-5, not a lead.
RELION-5 keeps the lead in both runs for FSC at 3.5\,\AA\ (0.276/0.277 vs.\ 0.243--0.266), band mean (0.244/0.244 vs.\ 0.226--0.234) and atomic agreement (3.86 vs.\ 4.04 and 4.01\,\AA).
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_runs.csv (fsc35, band, atomic05_A of both runs and job078)
\Cref{tab:suppl:resolution} lists the resolution after each stage.
Both runs end the ladder at 5.7\,\AA, the cold fit at 3.9--4.1\,\AA\ and cycle~1 at 3.37--3.43\,\AA.
% src: docs/paper/supp_timing_resolution.csv (step4_5.5: 5.671/5.667, 5.731/5.689; fit0: 3.904/4.063, 3.935/4.063; cycle1: 3.370/3.403, 3.372/3.433)
